2. **BLACKLISTED — Megaspeed cut off.** The US Commerce Department added
   Singapore-based Megaspeed and its affiliates to its Entity List on

   over its alleged role in diverting Nvidia chips to China. On Tuesday,
   Singapore PM Lawrence Wong said Singapore would keep investigating

   Sources: Wall Street Journal, 23 Nov 2026; Reuters ("US to blacklist
   Megaspeed", 23 Nov 2026; "Singapore investigating Nvidia-linked

3. **WARSHIP SUNK — Off Libya.** Ukrainian special forces launched seven
   explosive sea drones before dawn on Sunday 22 Nov against the Russian
   corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval base; the ship sank, a Ka-27
   helicopter aboard was destroyed, and the cargo vessel MV Angara was
   hit. President Zelenskyy claimed the operation on Monday 23 Nov.
   Sources: Wall Street Journal ("Inside the Ukrainian Attack That Sank
   Russian Warship Docked in Libya", 24 Nov 2026); Reuters Connect,
   23 Nov 2026; AP, 23 Nov 2026; CNN, 24 Nov 2026.
4. **FACTORY FIRE — 24 dead, 30 missing.** Indonesia confirmed on Monday
   that 24 people died in the fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy
   factory in Cikande, Serang, Banten — which broke out on Saturday
   21 Nov, possibly from an air-conditioner short circuit — with about
   30 workers still missing as rescuers searched the wreckage.
   Sources: Reuters (Jakarta, 23 Nov 2026); People ("At Least 24 People
   Dead After Fire Tears Through Candy Factory", 24 Nov 2026).
5. **PACIFIC GOES IT ALONE — After COP31 fails.** COP31 in Antalya ended
   on Sunday 22 Nov with no agreement — the first COP failure since
   Copenhagen 2009 — after the final draft omitted any mention of fossil
   fuels. Pacific island nations, led by Tuvalu and the Marshall Islands,
   refused the text and were left to form their own climate coalitions.
   Sources: PINA ("Pacific goes it alone as Bowen's COP31 talks
   collapse", 24 Nov 2026); AAP, 23 Nov 2026.
6. **EU SANCTIONS — 22nd package.** The European Commission proposed a
   22nd sanctions package against Russia on Monday, banning Russian teams
   and athletes from competing under the Russian flag in EU events and
   targeting banks, crypto platforms and ex-combatants.
   Sources: WH News ("EU Proposes 22nd Sanctions Package Against
   Russia", 24 Nov 2026).
